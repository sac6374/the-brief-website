Stocks Clobber as AI Doubts, Oil Spike Derail Tech Rally

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite finished Thursday's session lower, with the broad market index and tech-heavy Nasdaq losing 0.47% and 1.25% to 7,765.36 and 27,193.34, respectively. Shares of key tech names came under pressure after OpenAI's annualized revenue was revealed to be short of the amount previously signaled. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were lower on Thursday as investors faced more pressure from rising Treasury yields and elevated oil prices.

Key Points
  • Chip stocks declined after a Financial Times report cast doubt over AI developer OpenAI's revenue, with the company's annualized revenue $20 billion lower than previously estimated.
  • Shares of Oracle were down more than 5%, while chipmakers such as Nvidia and Advanced Micro Devices lost nearly 3% and almost 4%, respectively.
  • Wolfspeed shares surged more than 15% after the chipmaker secured a conditional $1.5 billion loan, marking a 30-year financing commitment from the Defense Department.
  • Oil prices pared gains after President Trump said the US would not be attacking Iran prior to the Midterm Elections, with Brent crude and WTI crude settling at $104 and $91 per barrel, respectively.
