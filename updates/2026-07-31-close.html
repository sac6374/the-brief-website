Tech surges into August as Amazon soars, rates hit 18-month highs

The S&P 500 rose 0.70% to 7,489.72, the Dow gained 0.53% to 52,485.03, and the Nasdaq rallied 1.00% to 25,373.85 on Friday. Amazon jumped 15% on strong earnings, while Apple plummeted 7.2% after disappointing China and Services results. The 10-year Treasury yield surged to 4.71% and the 30-year hit 5.249%.

Key Points
  • Big Tech giants reported continued investments in artificial intelligence, offsetting concern about rising bond yields.
  • The four hyperscalers (Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, and Alphabet) forecast spending $720 billion to $745 billion cumulatively on capital projects in 2026.
  • Oil prices turned higher as traffic in the Strait of Hormuz began to falter following a recent reescalation in hostilities, with WTI crude futures last trading at around $85 per barrel and Brent at $90.
  • The Dow Industrials was the only major index to post monthly gains in July as the Nasdaq-100 fell approximately 7% for the month, recording its steepest decline since March 2025.
