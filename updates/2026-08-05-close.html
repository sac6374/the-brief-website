Dow hits fresh record as market rotates from tech; SpaceX, AMD weigh sentiment

The Dow Jones Industrial Average increased 0.83% to close at 54,533.87 on August 5, 2026, supported by hopes of increased artificial intelligence spending. The S&P 500 posted a modest gain of 0.07%, while the Nasdaq declined by 0.49%. Investors repositioned away from expensive AI shares lacking immediate profits, looking for stocks with clear revenue and earnings growth.

Key Points
  • The Dow posted a fresh record close supported by hopes of increased artificial intelligence spending.
  • SpaceX fell 12%, despite posting a 92% jump in revenue, as newly unlocked shares hit the market. Nvidia added 4% after announcing a data-center chip agreement with SpaceX.
  • Netlist surged 46.5% after striking a $239 million upfront, five-year licensing deal with Samsung Electronics for Netlist's full patent portfolio. The agreement also provides for up to $897 million in contingent fees and could lead to $1.5 billion in Samsung memory purchases across five years.
  • Stocks rotated as investors assessed earnings and cheered diplomatic efforts for another peace deal between the US and Iran. Growing hopes that the Strait of Hormuz could soon reopen uplifted markets as earnings continued to roll in.
What to Watch

→ Earnings momentum continues after the 4:00 PM ET closing bell today, with several high-profile reports scheduled to drop. Investors will be closely watching Western Digital Corp. (WDC), which is expected to report earnings of $3.27 per share. As a key player in the storage and memory market, its results often serve as a bellwether for broader tech hardware demand.

→ Markets trimmed expectations for a September Federal Reserve rate hike to around 57%, down from 67% a day earlier.

→ SpaceX stock faces additional pressure this week when 20% of shares will be unlocked for trading.
