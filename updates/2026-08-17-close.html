Oil Surge on Iran Tensions Sends Stocks Lower; Retail Earnings Loom

U.S. stocks slipped on Monday as oil prices surged amid escalating U.S.-Iran tensions following the expiration of a 60-day ceasefire. The S&P 500 and Dow fell 0.5% while the Nasdaq declined 0.3%, trimming gains from three straight weeks of rallies. Retail earnings from Walmart, Target, and Home Depot dominate the week ahead.
