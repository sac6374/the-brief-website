The S&P 500 is sitting just 13 points off its all-time record close — but the macro backdrop heading into this week looks anything but record-worthy. July retail sales fell 0.6% (vs. +0.1% expected), consumer sentiment dropped to 51 from 55.2 in July, and the Fed remains deeply divided, with three dissenting votes at its last meeting in favor of an immediate rate hike. This week is when data meets earnings: Home Depot, Target, Lowe's, and Walmart all report, followed by FOMC minutes on Wednesday. Then the calendar flips to Jackson Hole (Aug 27–29), where Fed Chair Kevin Warsh speaks for the first time as chair.
