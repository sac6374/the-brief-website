Thursday's session answered a key question: is AI capex translating into real revenue? For Amazon, the answer is an emphatic yes — AWS grew 37% YoY in Q2, its fastest pace since 2021, and Amazon raised its 2026 capex plan to $220 billion. Apple told a different story: a record quarter undermined by a guidance miss and supply constraints that are forcing price hikes on Macs and iPads. Meanwhile, the Fed held rates at 3.5%–3.75% with three dissenters pushing for a hike — and the bond market is still not convinced inflation is under control. The 10-year yield is at its highest since January 2025.
