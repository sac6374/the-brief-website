September closed on a sour note — the S&P 500 erased a 0.7% intraday gain to finish down 0.25%, while the 10-year Treasury yield settled at 5.29%, its highest level since 2007. The culprit wasn't bad data. PCE inflation cooled more than expected, and ADP showed 90,000 jobs added in September. The problem is that good economic news at these yield levels means the Fed has room to stay restrictive longer — and markets are pricing exactly that. This morning, Micron's record fiscal Q4 ($54.23B in revenue, $33.42 EPS) is giving tech a lift in premarket, but gross margin guidance for the current quarter disappointed slightly, which is why shares are flat. Today brings ISM Manufacturing, jobless claims, and a wave of Fed speakers.
