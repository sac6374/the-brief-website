Wall Street's record-breaking week was built on a paradox: stocks surged because the economy shed 23,000 jobs in July — the first monthly payroll decline since February. The weak data slashed the odds of a September Fed rate hike to 44%, sending the S&P 500 to its fifth all-time high of the month. Meanwhile, Palantir rose 35% in five sessions after a blowout Q2 earnings beat, and SpaceX reported its first quarterly results as a public company. This week, all of it gets stress-tested: July CPI prints Wednesday, oil is rising again as Iran-Oman Hormuz talks stall, and premarket futures are barely moved.
